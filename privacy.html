Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

This site does not require accounts and does not run advertising or cross-site trackers. We collect as little as possible, and only what we need to respond to you.

What we collect

  • Contact details you share — if you write to us through the contact form or by email, we receive your name, email address and message.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) used to keep the site secure and reliable.

How we use information

We use the information you share only to respond to your message and, if relevant, to follow up on the conversation you started. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing you did not ask for.

Contact form delivery

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to us via FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Your name, email and message pass through their systems on the way to our inbox.

Cookies and analytics

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use cross-site analytics on this page.

Sharing

We share information only when required by law, or with service providers who help us operate this site (such as form delivery and hosting) and who are bound to handle it responsibly.

Data retention

We keep correspondence for as long as it is useful to the conversation, and delete it when it no longer is. Server logs are rotated regularly and not kept longer than necessary for security.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it. Write to contact@ozevon.com and we will respond.

Contact us

For anything privacy-related: contact@ozevon.com. For everything else: contact@ozevon.com.

Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
